What This Model Cannot Tell You

Known Limitations & Boundary Conditions

  • •
    Finds persistent heat, not confirmed factories. The algorithm scores thermal duration, regardless of whether that thermal signature originates from a smelter, gas flare, coal seam, or geothermal vent.
  • •
    16% database match does not disprove remainder. Only 16% of high-alert sites match known power registries; the remainder represent uncataloged industrial processing, not model false positives.
  • •
    Does not predict future fire spread. This system is strictly an ex-post behavioral filter on past orbital observations, not a real-time propagation forecast model.
  • •
    Training labels derived from heuristic clues. NASA FIRMS classification codes serve as empirical proxies rather than audited field ground-truth.

Designed to support analyst triage and automated pipeline dispatch, not replace field site confirmation or orbital radiometric telemetry inspection.
